Electrophysiology Primer: 10-20 Montage & ERP Selectivity

1. The International 10-20 System

The 10-20 system is an internationally recognized method to describe and apply the location of scalp electrodes. The numbers 10 and 20 refer to the fact that the distances between adjacent electrodes are either 10% or 20% of the total front-back or right-left distance of the skull.

2. Topography of the N170 ERP Component

The N170 is a prominent negative deflection occurring at 130–200ms post-stimulus. It is maximally recorded over parieto-occipitotemporal sites (P7, P8, PO7, PO8). It reflects the structural encoding of visual faces.

3. Channel Artifact Profiles

• Fp1 / Fp2 (Frontal): Highly sensitive to vertical eye blinks and saccades.
• T7 / T8 (Temporal): Susceptible to high-frequency jaw and facial muscle (EMG) noise.
• O1 / O2 / Oz (Occipital): Dominant baseline Alpha rhythm (8-12 Hz).
